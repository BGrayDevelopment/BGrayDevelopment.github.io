Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

The apps published under BGray Development are personal tools used only by their owner, Brenton Gray. They are not offered to other users.

What data is accessed

When the owner signs in with Google, the app can read the Google data the owner approves, such as their own Gmail messages or calendar, so a personal assistant can answer questions about them.

How it is used and stored

Data is used only to answer the owner's own requests. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The only thing kept is the sign-in token, stored on the owner's private home server. Use of data received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
